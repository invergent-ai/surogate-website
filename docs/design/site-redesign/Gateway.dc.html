Your ChatGPT and Claude plans

Control every call.

Who can call which model, what they can spend, where prompts may go, and what happens when a provider fails.

Access

Every caller gets its own key.

Issue a key per person, team or agent, and grant each one only the models it should use.

Spend

Spending stops at the limit.

Monthly limits per key, a reservation on every request, and exact accounting for every call.

Safety

Guardrails read the answer first.

Redact emails, card numbers, API keys and your own patterns, and run your injection and safety classifiers. Answers are checked too, and a blocked one never reaches the caller.

Data

Prompts go only where you allow.

Keep an organisation on providers that neither train on prompts nor keep them, or on EU-hosted ones, and decide whether prompts are logged at all.

Reliability

When a provider fails, the call moves on.

Route by health, price, speed or a fixed order. Slow or failing providers drop back, and a failed call moves on to the next one.

Visibility

See every call and what it cost.

Spend, tokens, latency and errors by model, provider and app, and each request's route from the first attempt to the answer.
