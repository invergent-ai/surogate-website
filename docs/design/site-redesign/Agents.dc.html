A tour of the Surogate platform

films/surogate-platform-tour.mp4, 1:24, added on the live site

From a blank page to a live agent in about ten minutes.

Every part of an agent is a screen in the studio, and the studio checks it as you go.

  1. 01

    Create

    Start from a template or a blank agent. It is running in about 30 seconds.

  2. 02

    Teach

    Attach skills and knowledge bases. Upload your documents and build them into a wiki.

  3. 03

    Govern

    Choose the tools it may use, the calls that need approval and the sites it may browse.

  4. 04

    Test

    Chat with it in the studio and see every tool call, file and decision.

  5. 05

    Publish

    Put it on Slack, Teams, WhatsApp, the phone or your website.

The Copilot can take any step for you.

It asks what the agent is for, shows a plan, and builds only what you approve. Anything that reaches people or money needs its own yes.

Run all of it inside your own network.

On-premise or air-gapped, the whole platform runs on your Kubernetes with your models, and no data has to leave your network.

Open-source runtime

The runtime is on GitHub. Your security team can read every line.

Your own models

Surogate 3.7, Jackrabbit and Amami run on your GPUs, next to your fine-tunes.

Your own hardware

Train and serve on your servers over SSH, or on your own cloud account.

Your keys and records

Credentials stay in your vault. Sessions, files and logs stay in your storage.

Every part of an agent, on its own screen.

Eight tabs describe an agent. Change one and the next session uses it, with no rollout.

Identity

The soul: who the agent is, how it speaks, what it refuses. Screened for prompt injection.

Skills

Procedures in markdown or drawn on the canvas. Every version is kept, and each session records the one it used.

Knowledge Bases

Your documents as a searchable wiki, with the source passage behind every answer.

Tools

MCP servers and more than 1,000 Composio toolkits, scanned before the agent sees them.

Model

Ours, your fine-tunes, OpenRouter or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

Capabilities

The browser, Claude Code and Codex, deep research, goals, missions and schedules.

Coding

Repositories the agent can branch, change and open pull requests on.

Governance

Allowed tools, approvals, browsing rules and the EU AI Act notice.

The runtime you would otherwise build yourself.

Sessions, sandboxes, credentials and rules are part of the platform, and the runtime is open source.

It keeps working

Sessions that survive crashes

If a worker dies mid-turn, another resumes from the log.

Saga rollback

When a step fails, finished steps are undone in reverse order.

A browser you can take over

Take control for a login or a CAPTCHA, then hand it back.

Office files, edited live

Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF beside the chat. Your edits win.

It stays inside the rules

Keys the agent never sees

The proxy adds credentials on the way out.

Rules fixed for each turn

Text injected mid-turn cannot loosen the tool policy.

Approvals

Sensitive calls wait in the inbox. A grant covers one call, for one hour.

Checked tools

Definitions are scanned for hidden instructions and fingerprinted against changes.
